    How it works

    1. List what you can teach and want to learn.
    2. Swap with a neighbor: 1 hour = 1 time credit.
    3. Share what you made and get feedback.
    Design notes: audience, color scheme, HCI, references

    Target audience

    Adults 18–45 in the same neighborhood or town: students and early-career workers who want experience and portfolio pieces, parents and hobbyists with practical skills, and people on tight budgets. Mixed digital confidence, mostly on phones. This is why every page has plain language, large touch targets, and a short path to “list a skill.”

    Color scheme

    Deep teal (trust, calm), warm amber (friendliness, call to action), off-white background, charcoal text. Pairs are chosen to meet WCAG 2.2 AA (4.5:1) and color is never the only signal: statuses and levels are always written out.

    HCI principles

    Sustainability: reuses local skills, light pages. Efficiency: short forms, few steps, balance and next step always visible. Resilience: readable on slow networks, plain HTML first, phone/SMS contact.
